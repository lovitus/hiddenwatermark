Privacy Policy

Hidden Watermark Master — last updated October 20, 2026

Summary

Hidden Watermark Master works entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data processed on your device

  • Photos you choose or capture are processed locally to add or detect invisible watermarks. They are never uploaded.
  • Camera is used only when you tap “Camera Shoot & Watermark”. Images are not stored by us.
  • Photo library access is used only to load the images you pick, and to save watermarked images when you choose to save them.
  • History records (dates, algorithm names, short text) are kept in local storage on your device. You can clear them at any time in the app.
  • Usage counts for the monthly free allowance are stored locally on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple provides us only with the information needed to confirm your subscription status.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: im@philgim.fr

隐私政策

隐形水印大师 — 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 20 日

概要

隐形水印大师完全在您的设备上运行。我们不收集、不传输、不出售或分享任何个人数据。应用没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告，也没有任何追踪。

在设备上处理的数据

  • 照片：您选择或拍摄的照片仅在本地处理，用于添加或检测隐形水印，绝不上传。
  • 相机：仅在您点击“相机拍照加水印”时使用。我们不保存图片。
  • 相册：仅用于读取您选择的图片，以及在您选择保存时写入加水印后的图片。
  • 历史记录（日期、算法名称、简短文字）保存在设备本地存储中，您可以随时在应用内清空。
  • 免费额度计数保存在设备本地。

购买

订阅由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们不会收到您的支付信息，Apple 只向我们提供确认订阅状态所需的信息。

儿童

应用不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。
